Our Mission

"Bringing back the good old reggae — timeless roots, rocksteady, lovers rock & 80s dancehall."

Most die-hard reggae lovers find it harder every year to hear authentic, golden-era Jamaican music played with care. Cool & Deadly Reggae was built by veteran selectors for listeners who still treasure the soul of Bob Marley, Dennis Brown, Beres Hammond, Alton Ellis, Gregory Isaacs, and Delroy Wilson.

Whether you're tuning in for a live weekend sound system session or cruising through our 24/7 curated rotation, you're home at coolanddeadlyreggae.com.
